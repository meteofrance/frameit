Instructional analytical inputs
===============================

Generate the inputs with :doc:`../task_guides/generate_fixtures`.
All fields are prescribed teaching data, not balanced simulations or validated
producer files. ``stationary`` fixes the centre; ``data`` moves it east;
``missing-data`` adds gaps and categories.

Atmosphere
----------

The atmosphere has a 25×25 latitude/longitude grid with 0.05° spacing and samples
at hours 0, 1 and 2 on 1 January 2000. The centre starts at 60°E, 20°S.
The translating case increases longitude by 0.1° per hour at constant latitude.
This prescribed path does not impose a constant metric translation speed.

Let :math:`r` be WGS84 geodesic distance from that time's centre. The signed
tangential wind and sea-level pressure are:

.. math::

   V_\theta(r) = (40\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}})
      \frac{r}{40\,\mathrm{km}}
      \exp\left[\frac{1-(r/40\,\mathrm{km})^2}{2}\right],

   p(r) = 101000\,\mathrm{Pa}
      - (5000\,\mathrm{Pa})\exp[-(r/40\,\mathrm{km})^2].

Positive tangential velocity is clockwise. Local east/north components
use the terminal-forward geodesic radial bearing; wind is zero at the centre.
Velocity magnitude peaks at 40 km. Pressure is minimal at the centre.

``calculated-track/atmosphere.nc`` uses the translating vortex. It stores
the same surface fields as ``prmsl``, ``u10`` and ``v10``. The winds are
labelled at 10 m. The pressure is 96000 Pa at each known centre.

The atmospheric layer heights are 10, 1000 and 3000 m. Their instructional
temperature is :math:`300\,\mathrm K-(0.006\,\mathrm{K\,m^{-1}})z`.
Interfaces at 0, 500, 2000 and 4000 m carry :math:`w=(0.001\,\mathrm{s^{-1}})z`.

Forecast archive
----------------

``forecast-archive/`` has three 7×7 pressure files on a 0.01° grid. On
1 January 2000, the 00:00 start has leads 1 h and 2 h with constant values
100100 and 100200 Pa. The 01:00 start has a 1 h lead with 101200 Pa. Its valid
time is also 02:00. The names put ``z-earlier.nc`` after ``a-later.nc`` in
alphabetical order; the valid times put it first.

Pressure-level and interface exercises
--------------------------------------

``pressure.nc`` has pressure labels 85000, 70000 and 50000 Pa on ``isobaric``.
At zero-based time/horizontal indices :math:`t,j,i`, its instructional
temperature is

.. math::

   T = 270\,\mathrm K + (0.0002\,\mathrm{K\,Pa^{-1}})p
       + (1\,\mathrm K)t + (0.1\,\mathrm K)(j-12+i-12).

``height_temperature`` uses the height formula above.
``interfaces.nc`` retains the same temperature/W arrays, with Meso-NH-labelled
``level/level_w`` and computational axes at 5000 m spacing, for the coordinate-linear
interface remap.

``staggered-winds/mesonh.nc`` has 9×9 mass points 1 km apart, with faces offset
by half a cell. The selected native level is labelled 5 m. On the x faces,
``UT = 10 + x/1000`` m/s for ``x`` in metres. ``VT`` is zero on the y faces.
The synthetic grid's x direction points north; its y direction points west.
The angle is π/2 radians. At the centre the collocated eastward and
northward components are 0 and 10 m/s.

Three-time coupled illustration
-------------------------------

The separate ``coupled-atmosphere.nc`` and ``coupled-ocean.nc`` files share hours
0, 1 and 2. Original tutorial files and their independent clocks are unchanged.
Winds reuse the translating vortex and are declared at 10 m for this illustration.
Air temperature adds a prescribed warm anomaly:

.. math::

   T_a = 300 - 0.006z + 2\exp[-(r_t/40000)^2]\exp(-z/1000).

Lengths are in metres and temperatures in kelvin. Ocean temperature retains the
largest cooling prescribed at or before each time:

.. math::

   T_o = 300 + 0.01z
       - 2\max_{k\le t}\{\exp[-(r_k/15000)^2]\}\exp(z/20).

At ``z=0`` this is the illustrated SST. The persistent cold trail is defined by
this formula, not generated by air–sea dynamics or validated against observations.

Coupled clocks
--------------

In ``coupled-clocks/``, the 9×9 AROME grid has 0.05° spacing and the
7×7 NEMO grid has 0.08° spacing. Both are centred at 20°S, 60°E.
For zero-based time, row, and column indices ``n, j, i``, pressure is
``100000 + 100*n + 10*j + i`` Pa. Potential temperature at 5 m depth is
``290 + n + 0.1*j + 0.01*i`` K.

The pressure samples and track centres have times 06:00, 07:00, and 08:00
on 1 January 2000. Track longitude advances 0.05° per hour. NEMO has means
over 05:00–06:00 and 07:00–08:00, labelled at their midpoints. There is
no sample for the hour ending at 07:00.

Original ocean fixtures
-----------------------

The ocean uses a coarser 13×13 rho-labelled grid with 0.1° spacing and its own
instantaneous samples at hours 0 and 2. At each ocean time it uses the same
prescribed centre as the atmosphere:

.. math::

   T(r,z,t) = 300\,\mathrm K + (0.01\,\mathrm{K\,m^{-1}})z
     - (2\,\mathrm K)\exp[-(r/40\,\mathrm{km})^2]\exp[z/(20\,\mathrm m)].

Here :math:`z` is height relative to the sea surface, at −30, −10 and 0 m.
The synthetic CROCO convention uses ``Vtransform=2``, ``s_rho=Cs_rho``
equal to ``[-0.75, -0.25, 0]``, bathymetry 40 m, critical depth 5 m and
zero surface elevation. Its declared formula therefore gives
:math:`z=40\,\mathrm m\,s_\rho`.
The surface-labelled rho sample is an instructional choice, not a claim of
CROCO producer conformance or ocean-response physics.

Coverage and verification
-------------------------

The baseline fields are finite. The ``missing`` variant sets atmospheric U/V
to missing at zero-based horizontal index ``(10, 11)``. Its ``surface_type``
has valid integer categories 0 (water) and 1 (land); categorical sampling requires
nearest-neighbour interpolation. The ocean's first row has a static land mask
and missing temperature. Missing samples are never replaced by physical zeroes.

Tests compare wind, pressure and temperature against direct formula evaluations
and reopen actual native/polar products with ordinary readers. Formula comparisons
use a 10⁻¹² absolute tolerance for float64 calculations. Interpolation has its
own error and coverage behavior; point sampling is not an area-budget calculation.
These checks establish the instructional definitions, not scientific validity
for real atmospheric or ocean-model outputs.

Each file is published atomically and existing paths are refused. The generated
set is not a transaction: if generation fails part-way, retain that directory
for inspection and retry in a fresh directory rather than mixing attempts.
